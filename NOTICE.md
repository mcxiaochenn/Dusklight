# Third-party notices

## MiSans

Dusklight embeds **MiSans** for site UI and article text. The tracked WOFF2 files are Unicode subsets generated from the user-supplied `MiSansVF.ttf` with `scripts/subset-misans.py`; the original ZIP/TTF is not redistributed in this repository. MiSans is provided by Xiaomi. See the official license and FAQ: https://hyperos.mi.com/font/zh/faq/
